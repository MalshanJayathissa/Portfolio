Projects

Traffic Fine Management System

Traffic enforcement agencies must implement more intelligent and effective procedures to handle fines and penalties due to the growing number of cars on the road and the increasing number of traffic infractions. Conventional manual methods for managing and administering traffic penalties are frequently opaque, labor-intensive, and prone to human mistake. Because they rely on paperwork and in-person visits to resolve penalties, they also place a heavy strain on both residents and law enforcement. The Traffic Fine Management System was created as a software solution to address these issues and modernize the procedure by facilitating digital tracking and administration of traffic infractions.
